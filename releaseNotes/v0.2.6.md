# Black Duck-shaped export matches the transformed reports

- `export-blackduck` now writes `_dependencies.csv`, `_dependencies_sources.csv` and `_upgrade_guidance.csv` with the exact header line and cell conventions `transformBlackDuckReports` produces, and adds `_vulnerability_details.csv` (the transform's 23-column view of `security.csv`). A downstream reader processes a depinder folder and a transformed Black Duck folder the same way.
- Headers and cell rules moved to one shared module, `src/blackduck/columns.ts`, imported by both commands.
- Cell conventions in the shareable files: `Match type` is `Direct` / `Transitive` / `Direct,Transitive`; a zero per-severity count is blank while `Total` and `Critical and High` stay numeric sums; dates are `\tYYYY-MM-DD`; `Version id` is empty; `VerifiedPathMethod` is `not-checked`; files end with a newline.
- `1Component name` and `ProjectPathExists` are gone; `Version id` and `VerifiedPathMethod` are in.
- `Newer Versions (semver)` left the Black Duck-shaped files for the new depinder-only `_component_versions.csv`.
- `addCategoriesToBlackDuckReports` joins on `Component Version Origin Id` when every `Version id` is empty, so it also works on an `export-blackduck` folder.
- `security.csv` is unchanged.
